# KromaTiD

> KromaTiD is a Boulder, Colorado single-cell analysis life-science tools and services
> company (founded 2016) built around directional Genomic Hybridization (dGH) — a
> hybridization chemistry that probes only the parental strand of DNA, resolving
> orientation-specific structural variation (inversions, translocations, insertions)
> cell by cell at resolutions NGS and conventional FISH cannot reach. Sold to cell and
> gene therapy developers as the KROMASURE platform, as kits, and as lab services.

## Platform and products

- [KROMASURE platform](https://kromatid.com/kromasure-platform): the fluorescent hybridization platform the product lines sit on.
- [dGH in-Site](https://kromatid.com/insite): targeted single-cell structural variant detection at a chosen locus.
- [dGH SCREEN](https://kromatid.com/screen): unbiased whole-genome analysis of chromosomal structural rearrangements.
- [kBand](https://kromatid.com/kband): banding-based chromosomal integrity analysis.
- [PinPoint](https://kromatid.com/kromasure-pinpoint): high-throughput transgene integration analysis at single-cell resolution.
