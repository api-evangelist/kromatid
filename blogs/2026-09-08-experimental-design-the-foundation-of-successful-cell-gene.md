In cell and gene therapy, every decision made during development has the potential to impact patient safety, product quality, regulatory success, and ultimately, commercial viability. While tremendous attention is often placed on vector design, editing efficiency, and manufacturing processes, one critical component is sometimes overlooked: thoughtful experimental design.
